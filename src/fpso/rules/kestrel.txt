# Logistics rule cards for FPSO Kestrel (mock), one rule per line, as the planners wrote them.
No single item over 150 kg goes by helicopter.
Each passenger counts as 110 kg of helicopter payload.
No cargo is lifted on a bunkering day.
Cargo is lifted only when a crane is available.
Cargo needs 1 day at the shore base before it sails.
A sailing takes 1 day to reach the asset.
A transfer from the sister asset takes 7 days.
Keep 2% of the deck space free for emergency cargo.
# Phase 3: weather limits and what agents may do without asking.
No sailing when the wave height is over 4.5 m.
Helicopters do not fly when the wind is over 50 knots.
Actions under 5000 USD may run without approval.
